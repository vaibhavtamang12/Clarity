## Standing Interpretation

RRF is the production default (D-045): rank-based fusion is insensitive to
the scale mismatch between cosine similarity and lexical scores. Weighted
fusion is retained as an experiment arm because its sensitivity to score
distributions is exactly what benchmarks should expose.
