# Incident Response Plan

This plan defines the incident response process for security and operational incidents.

## Incident Reporting

Security incidents must be reported to security@company.com within 1 hour of discovery.
Operational incidents are reported via PagerDuty to the on-call engineer.
All incidents are classified by severity: P1 (critical), P2 (high), P3 (medium), P4 (low).

## Response Process

P1 incidents require immediate response (within 15 minutes) and a war room call.
P2 incidents must be acknowledged within 30 minutes.
P3 and P4 incidents are addressed during business hours.

## Postmortem

A postmortem is required within 5 business days for all P1 and P2 incidents.
The postmortem must include: timeline, root cause, impact, action items.
Postmortems are reviewed by the engineering leadership team.
